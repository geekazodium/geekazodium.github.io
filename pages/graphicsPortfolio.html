Water Shader

The water effects in this project are probably the most advanced part of the project. Here's the rundown.

Displacement: this is done by summing up a bunch of sine waves at different scales, and having the waves move forward seperately.

Ambient Color: the lighting model for the ambient color is computed seperately from specular, and does not account for the light soure, it instead blends the colors of 3 different factors. elevation, the higher the vertical offset, the more likely it is for light to bounce back out. view normal, less light reflects from water into the camera when looking at a grazing angle. ambient a constant value used to ensure not full darkness.

The above ambient model is highly inspired by the implemenatation of sea of thieves.

However, this produces water that fakes some degree of photorealism, which does not match the stylized aim. to achieve the stylization, the luminance gets a noise texture added to it and then posterized (but not the actual base color), and multiplied with the normalized (by luminance) based color. this achieves a posterized look, but preserves slight differences in hue and saturation, as well as removing issues with the luminance resulting in color banding.

The specular component of the lighting uses a standard reflected light equation with anisotrophy to more realistically replicate water's reflective properties. This brightness is theasholded to make the lighting produce hard edges, creating an effect more fitting of pixelart.
